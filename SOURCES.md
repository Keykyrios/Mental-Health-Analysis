# Data sources

| File | What | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data/ncrb_student_suicides_national.csv` | Student suicides in India, 2014-2024 | NCRB, *Accidental Deaths & Suicides in India 2024* (released May 2026), as reported by [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/student-suicide-ncrb-report-2026-rise-80-pc-10-years-1000-under-18-jobs-unemployment-exam-mental-health-school-higher-education/amp) | Police-recorded; likely an undercount. Analysis uses 2023 and 2024 only. Tamil Nadu 2024 qualified printed as "8,9198", corrected to 89,198. |
| `data/population_census2011.csv` | State population, Census 2011 | [Puducherry Directorate of Economics and Statistics](https://statistics.py.gov.in/statewise-population-2011) | 2011 base understates 2024 population, so density is overstated in every state. Ranking is more reliable than level. Andhra Pradesh and Telangana are combined, as are J&K and Ladakh. States under 10 lakh people are dropped from per-capita charts. |
| `data/ncrb_student_suicides_top_states.csv` | Student suicides, four large states, 2021-2024 | [India TV](https://indiatvnews.com/news/india/india-student-suicide-rate-surges-list-of-states-with-most-such-cases-population-growth-rate-maharashtra-tamil-nadu-madhya-pradesh-uttar-pradesh-2024-08-29-949448), [Careers360 2023](https://news.careers360.com/ncrb-report-2023-over-13800-student-suicide-deaths-highest-in-10-years-national-crime-records-bureau-maharashtra-up-tn/amp), [Careers360 2024](https://news.careers360.com/student-suicide-ncrb-report-2026-rise-80-pc-10-years-1000-under-18-jobs-unemployment-exam-mental-health-school-higher-education/amp) | Only four states reported in the press. Kept for reference, not charted. |
